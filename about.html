About StockIntro

StockIntro is an educational website built to make stock market investing accessible to everyone. No finance degree required.

Learn

Clear, jargon-free guides covering everything from what a stock is to how to read a candlestick chart.

Grow

Practical how-to guides and curated resources so you can go from reading to actually doing it.

Stay Safe

Honest coverage of risks, emotional pitfalls, and fees. Informed investors make better decisions.

Who We Are

StockIntro is an academic project created as part of a Web Design module. It was built by a first-year student with a passion for making financial literacy more accessible.

This site is not affiliated with any broker, financial institution, or investment firm. No content on this site constitutes financial advice.
